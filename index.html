FIELD GUIDE

How to play

Clear each room of rats. Everything moves when you do, so there’s no rush.

Hints follow the input you use. Open ☰ and change On-screen controls: shown / hidden to show or hide the pad. Your choice is saved, and switching devices keeps your chosen layout.

Directional pad
Move in four directions. Your sword turns with you. Hold to keep moving. The center of the pad is inactive.
Wait
Pass one turn without moving. In a two-hero room, both heroes wait.
Undo / Restart
Undo takes back moves. Tap Restart twice within two seconds to start the room again.
Two heroes
Hero controls appear only in rooms with two heroes. Tap a portrait to select the blue or orange hero; the matching ring marks them on the board. Turn on Move together, choose one hero’s direction, switch portraits, then choose the other’s direction. Turn it off to clear queued moves and return to ordinary moves. Wait makes both heroes wait.
Map & menus
Tap the game’s buttons and menu items directly; drag the map to explore. Play and Enter room appear on the title and map screens. After a win, tap Continue on the win card. Undo stays in its usual position.
Settings & control sides
Open ☰ to change settings. Drag the Music, Sound, and Screen shake bars, or tap a point on a bar. Tap D-pad: left or D-pad: right to swap the pad and action buttons; your preference is saved in this browser. Swipe the settings list to reach more options on smaller screens.
Room tips
Tips appear above the controls without moving or resizing the puzzle. Scroll a long tip to read it all. Tap − to minimize it, then Room tip to reopen it.
More room
The game removes its duplicate portrait instruction panel while the on-screen controls are visible. Set On-screen controls to hidden in ☰ to hide the pad and restore the game's own buttons. The settings panel scrolls when its options need more room.
Move / Wait
Use the arrow keys or WASD to move. Space passes a turn. Everything waits until you act.
Undo / Restart
U takes back moves. Press R twice within two seconds to restart.
Two heroes
Without a controller, arrow keys move the blue hero and WASD moves the orange hero. With a controller connected, both key sets move the orange hero. Hold right Shift with arrows or left Shift with WASD to queue a synchronized move. Space makes both heroes wait.
Menus / Map
Escape opens the menu. Use directions to choose an item and Enter to confirm, or click directly. Drag the map to explore. Settings sliders support dragging.
Move / Wait
Use the D-pad or left stick to move. The bottom or right face button passes a turn. The game shows the matching button symbols for your controller.
Undo / Restart
The left or top face button undoes a move. Press the left shoulder button or left trigger twice within two seconds to restart.
Two heroes
The first controller moves the blue hero. A second controller or the keyboard moves the orange hero. Hold the right shoulder button to queue a synchronized move, then choose a move for the other hero.
Menus / Map
Start opens the menu. Use the D-pad or stick to navigate and the bottom face button to confirm. Directions also adjust settings sliders.
Your progress

Completed rooms and settings are saved in this browser.
